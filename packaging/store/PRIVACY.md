# DDS-120 Scope privacy statement

DDS-120 Scope does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

- It makes no network connections.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- It talks only to the SainSmart DDS-120 oscilloscope connected to your device by USB.
- Its settings, and any CSV files or screenshots you choose to save, stay on your device.

Questions: use the support contact on the app's Microsoft Store page.
